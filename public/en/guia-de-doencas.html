Clinical Disease & Evidence-Based Guidelines

⚠️ Educational content only. Does not replace professional medical advice. Always consult a certified healthcare professional.

The clinical reference database of MediVille Manager is based on authentic primary healthcare and emergency medicine standards.

Dengue Fever without Warning Signs

Symptoms: Sudden high fever, retro-orbital pain, severe myalgia and arthralgia.

Guideline: Early oral rehydration therapy (60 mL/kg/day), paracetamol for fever. Strict contraindication for NSAIDs and aspirin.

Hypertensive Urgency

Symptoms: Blood pressure >= 180/120 mmHg with severe headache but no acute target organ damage.

Guideline: Oral captopril under observation with controlled ambulatory follow-up.
