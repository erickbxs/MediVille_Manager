Hospital Administrator Manual: Controls & Gameplay Rules

Navigation Controls

  • Horizontal Scroll: Rotate your mouse wheel over the build menu, press Arrow Left/Right, or grab and drag.
  • Pan Campus View: Click and drag on open hospital grounds or press W, A, S, D.
  • Rotate Room: Press R prior to confirming module placement.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does hospital reputation work?
Reputation is recalculated at midnight according to patient satisfaction. Dropping below 10 for 7 consecutive days triggers an emergency shutdown.
How do I master clinical protocols?
Correctly prescribing the official guideline in the consultation room permanently trains your clinical staff to resolve future matching cases automatically.
